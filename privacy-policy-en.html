Traforet ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is developed and maintained by an individual developer, Stanislav Suslin. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

1. Summary

Traforet is a photo editing app that works entirely on your device. We do not operate any servers, we do not collect your photos, and we do not require you to create an account. Almost everything the App does happens locally on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be direct about this: Traforet does not collect, transmit, store on any server, or have any access to:

  • Your photos, images, or any content you create or edit in the App
  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your contacts
  • Your location
  • Any account or login information (the App does not require or support account creation)

3. How Photos Are Handled

When you use the Stencil (camera overlay) feature, the camera feed and any reference photo you load are processed only in your device's memory while the App is open.

When you edit a photo (crop, filters, corrections), the image and your edit history exist only in memory during that editing session.

When you save or export a photo, it is written directly to your device's system Photos library — the same place any other photo app saves images. The App does not keep a separate copy anywhere.

When the App is closed, or when you switch to editing a different photo, this in-memory data (the photo itself, undo/redo history, filter preview thumbnails) is cleared. The App does not write your photos or edit history to its own storage on disk.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The only information the App retains between launches is two small technical settings, stored locally using Apple's standard UserDefaults system:

  • Whether you have purchased the Premium unlock (isPremium)
  • Whether you have already seen the Stencil feature's introduction screen (hasSeenTraforetOnboarding)

These are simple true/false flags. They do not identify you personally and contain no content, photos, or personal information. They are stored only on your device and are removed if you delete the App.

5. Permissions the App Requests

Camera — required for the Stencil (reference overlay) feature, so you can see the live camera feed. Camera data is processed only in memory and is never saved or transmitted unless you choose to take a photo, in which case it is saved to your Photos library as described above.

Photos Library — required so you can select a reference image or an existing photo to edit, and so the App can save your edited results back to your library.

The App only requests access to features it actively uses. You can review or change these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app.

6. In-App Purchases

Traforet offers one optional one-time purchase that unlocks additional filter categories. This purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details, billing information, or card data — this is handled exclusively by Apple under Apple's own privacy practices. You can restore a previous purchase at any time using the "Restore Purchases" option in the App.

7. Third-Party Code Used in the App

The App's filter and editing engine is built using the following open-source libraries, which run entirely on your device as part of the App's code. They do not operate as external services, do not collect data, and do not communicate with any server:

  • Mantis (MIT License) — used for the crop/transform tool
  • Harbeth (MIT License) — used for the photo filter engine

Neither library sends any data off your device.

8. Analytics

We do not integrate any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs (such as Firebase, Google Analytics, Meta/Facebook SDK, or similar). The App does not show ads.

Apple itself automatically collects aggregated, anonymized usage and crash data from all apps distributed through the App Store, as part of Apple's own platform-level services (App Store Connect Analytics). This collection is governed by Apple's own privacy practices, not by us, and we only ever see this data in aggregated, anonymized form, if at all.

9. Children's Privacy

Traforet is rated 4+ and is suitable for a general audience. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the App does not collect personal data from any user, no age-specific data collection occurs.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any personal data to begin with.

11. Your Rights

Since the App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us, there is no personal data held by us for you to access, correct, or delete. Any data related to the App (such as your edited photos) is stored entirely on your own device, under your own control, through your device's Photos app and Settings.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's functionality changes in a way that affects data handling. The current version will always be available at the link provided in the App, with the effective date updated accordingly.
